### 👨‍💻 About Me

Backend & AI Engineer specialized in designing **scalable distributed systems**, **autonomous multi-agent workflows**, and **high-throughput APIs**. I bridge the gap between production-grade backend engineering (**Go**, **PHP**, **Python**) and applied Artificial Intelligence—taking LLMs, RAG architectures, and ML models from prototype to resilient production infrastructure.

- 🧠 **AI & Agentic Systems:** Designing stateful multi-agent architectures, tool-calling pipelines, semantic search (RAG), and fine-tuned models in **Python**.
- ⚡ **High-Performance Backend:** Building low-latency microservices and concurrent workers in **Go**, alongside robust REST/GraphQL backends and enterprise systems in **PHP (Laravel/Symfony)** and **FastAPI**.
- 🛠️ **Focus Areas:** Agent Orchestration • Vector Databases • Distributed Systems • MLOps & LLM Evaluation.
